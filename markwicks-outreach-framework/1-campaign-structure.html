Who is the
contact?
TYPE A
Named decision-maker
Relevant individual identified.
Personalised email for their business and role.
Email and phone follow-up where appropriate.
TYPE B
General company inbox
e.g. info@company.com.au
Ask who handles grounds maintenance or contractor procurement.
Follow up to reach the right person.
TYPE C
Other employee / referral
May not be the decision-maker.
Ask if they handle the service or can point us to the right person.
Then continue with that contact.
Every contact type enters the same baseline sequence — only the opening message and the ask are tailored to who they are.
